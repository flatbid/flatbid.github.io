How it works

Version one works with the bulk files Amazon Ads already gives you. Automatic daily bidding through the Amazon Ads API comes next.

Step 1

Download your bulk file

In Amazon Ads → Bulk operations, export Sponsored Products for the last 30–60 days with search term data.

Step 2

Upload it to FlatBid

Set your target ACoS per product or campaign. FlatBid computes new bids, negatives and keyword harvests.

Step 3

Review and upload back

See every change with its reason, uncheck anything you disagree with, download the file and upload it to Amazon.

What FlatBid does

The bidding engine behind FlatBid already manages ad bids for sellers on other marketplaces. We are bringing it to Amazon.

Target-ACoS bid ladder

Raises bids on targets below your ACoS goal, lowers them above it, in measured steps with floors and caps you control.

Bid-raise check

Every bid increase is measured: week after vs week before. If it did not pay off, the bid is rolled back and the target is not boosted again for two weeks.

Negative keyword mining

Finds search terms that spend without converting and proposes them as negatives — only when several signals agree.

Keyword harvesting

Moves converting search terms from auto and broad campaigns into exact-match targets.

Account-level guardrail

If total ACoS drifts above your limit, FlatBid stops raising bids first and trims the most expensive targets — without pausing your ads.

A reason for every change

No black box. Each bid change comes with the numbers behind it, so you can learn from it or overrule it.

FAQ

Do you need access to my Seller Central or Amazon Ads account?

No. Version one works only with the bulk file you export yourself. You review every change and upload the file back to Amazon. The future Autopilot plan will connect through Amazon's official Ads API, only with your explicit consent, and you can revoke it at any time.

Is FlatBid available now?

Not yet. We are building it with our first sellers. Join early access and we will email you when your account is ready — usually with a free audit of your current campaigns first.

Why a flat price instead of a percentage of ad spend?

A percentage rewards the tool when you spend more. A flat price means our only incentive is to make your existing spend work harder.

Which ad types and marketplaces are supported?

We start with Sponsored Products on the US marketplace. Sponsored Brands, Sponsored Display and other marketplaces will follow based on what early users need most.

What happens to the files I upload?

They are used only to compute your recommendations and are never shared or sold. You can delete your data at any time. See the privacy notice.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes — in one click from your account. Unused days of a paid month are refunded.
