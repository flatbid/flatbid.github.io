Privacy notice

Last updated: October 26, 2026

What we collect

When you request early access we store what you enter in the form: your email, monthly ad spend range, the PPC tool you use, your optional comment, whether you are open to beta testing, and where you came from (for example, a link from a forum). We also keep the date of the request.

How we use it

  • To contact you about FlatBid early access, beta testing and launch.
  • To understand which sellers need FlatBid most, so we build the right features first.

We do not sell or share your information with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Files you upload later

If you join the beta and share an Amazon Ads bulk file, it is used only to compute recommendations for you. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

How long we keep it

Until you ask us to delete it, or 24 months after our last contact with you, whichever comes first.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from any email we send, and ask us to see, correct or delete your data by replying to any email from us.
